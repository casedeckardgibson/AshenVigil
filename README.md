You play Lira, a spellblade in a moonlit ruin, seen from a locked over-the-shoulder camera. She's modelled from primitives rather than sprites, so the character is a stylized 3D figure. The level uses real physics: you can climb stepped platforms, and a crate pyramid will collapse if you push into it.

Enemies: three roaming guardians patrol and chase you when you get close, and a boss waits on the dais.
Combat: when an enemy catches you, the game cuts to a locked camera behind your shoulder and switches to turn-based fighting. You pick Strike, Arcane Bolt, Guard or Potion with the mouse or the 1–4 keys. Enemies sometimes charge a heavy attack, and there are crits, damage numbers and screen shake.
Dialogue: the opening scene has a choice that gives a small stat bonus, each guardian speaks before its fight, and the boss has a short ending.
Controls: WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, and mouse (click to capture) or Q/E for the camera. In dialogue, Space or a click advances.
